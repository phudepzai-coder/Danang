01 / The story

From a classroom exercise
to a photo journal.

Michigan, on foot began with the three-page Michigan walks exercise provided by Colleen van Lent for the University of Michigan’s Web Design for Everybody courses. The photographs include flowering neighborhood streets, woodland paths, and walks with Bacon the Labradoodle.

This capstone adaptation adds an original editorial layout, a responsive gallery, and custom interactions while keeping the original materials clearly credited. It is a learning project developed with AI assistance. It is not a claim that the photographs were taken by the student.

02 / Ways to explore

A little more than a gallery.

01

Search and filter

Search photograph titles and descriptions, switch between the two collections, and see the result count update as you type.

02

A personal collection

Save photographs and return to them in the Saved collection. Your choices are stored locally in this browser; no account is needed.

03

A closer look, by keyboard

The enlarged viewer supports Previous and Next, Left and Right arrow keys, and Escape to close. Focus returns to the photograph you opened.

04

Your preferred light

Switch between light and dark mode on any page. Your choice is remembered in this browser, with readable colors in both modes.

The layout also changes across phone, tablet, and desktop widths. A flex-column page keeps the footer at the bottom of shorter pages.

03 / Access for everyone

Good design includes you.

Start with the Skip to main content link, navigate with Tab and Shift+Tab, and use visible focus outlines to keep your place. Every photograph has a description, filters expose their selected state, and result counts are announced without moving focus.

The site respects reduced-motion preferences. There is no autoplay, no tracking, and no form that sends your data to a server. If browser storage is unavailable, search and the photo viewer still work, and your preferences last for the current page.

04 / Sources and credits

Give credit. Keep learning.

Original Michigan walks HTML content and photographs: Colleen van Lent, University of Michigan, supplied in the Web Design for Everybody coursework. Source materials are retained in the original course exercise.

Capstone adaptation with AI-assisted HTML, CSS, JavaScript, documentation, and testing. The interface uses system fonts and no external framework or JavaScript plugin. Photographs are resized and encoded as WebP for faster loading; their content is unchanged.
